Claude course

The course goes from the basics to Claude Code: what Claude is, how to use it safely, how to write a good prompt, and how Claude Code helps in day-to-day administration and development. The course has 12 modules in four sessions. Every module has a core that is covered in the session and an optional part that you can do afterwards at your own pace.

Modules 0-5 work with a free Claude account. Claude Code needs a paid account, so modules 6-10 are demos by the instructor that Pro users can follow on their own machine. Paid features are marked, and their exercises have an alternative: follow the demo or work with a partner. The exercises are everyday administration: PowerShell, logs, error messages, regex, ticket replies, instructions and CSV files.
